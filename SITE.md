## Portfolio entry

**Title:** Testing the CAPM and the Fama-French factor models through 2026

**Type and year:** Open data analysis in Python, 2026

**Text:** A redo of a graduate financial economics exercise on data through September 2026. It reproduces the original result, then tests the CAPM, three- and five-factor models with GRS, Fama-MacBeth and Hansen-Jagannathan distance. All three are rejected on size and value portfolios. The factors fit those portfolios much better than beta does, but on industry portfolios they make the alphas larger.

**Links:** Project page, and code at https://github.com/atamalfuzi/capm_factor_tests
